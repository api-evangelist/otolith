# Otolith (Otolith Labs, Inc.)

> Otolith Labs is a privately held, clinical-stage medical device company in Washington,
> D.C., developing the OtoBand — a non-invasive wearable headband that applies calibrated
> mechanical vibration to the vestibular system ("non-invasive Vestibular Resonance
> Therapy", nVRT, also described as vestibular masking) to reduce the symptoms of chronic
> vertigo and vestibular migraine without surgery, medication, or patient-specific
> calibration.

## What this company is

- Founded 2015 by Sam Owen; based at 200 Massachusetts Ave NW, Floor 7, Washington, DC 20001.
- Sole product line: the OtoBand wearable, built on the company's nVRT / vestibular-masking
  technology. It holds an FDA Breakthrough Device designation and is a PRESCRIPTION,
  investigational device — not cleared for general commercial sale.
- Clinical program spans 600+ subjects with no reported serious adverse events, plus an
  at-home telehealth vertigo study; the technology has also been studied for motion
  sickness (validated with Jaguar Land Rover in 2018) and for VR cybersickness.
- Backed by Morningside Ventures ($20M Series A), Mark Cuban Companies, a $3.3M seed round
  and a group of ENT physician investors.
- Leadership: Dan Wagner (CEO), Kevin Franck (Chief Clinical Officer), Jon Akers (CTO,
  co-founder), Didier Depireux (Chief Science Officer, co-founder), Sam Owen (founder).
